{{platformName}} (the “Platform”) takes the protection of your personal information seriously. This policy explains what we collect, why, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have when you use the management console and related services. Please read it in full.

A note on roles: this policy covers administrators, who deal with the Platform directly. For end users of an integrator's application, the integrator determines the purposes and means of processing and acts as controller; the Platform acts only as processor on the integrator's instructions. End users should direct requests to that integrator.

1. What We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account information — account name, password (stored only as an irreversible hash), display name.
  • Contact details — email address and phone number, used for sign-in verification, security notices and receipt delivery. These are optional and basic use does not depend on them.
  • Profile information — avatar, date of birth, biography and contact entries you choose to add.
  • Authentication credentials — two-factor secrets (encrypted), recovery codes (hashed only), passkey public keys. We never store your passkey private keys or biometric data; those stay on your device.
  • Tickets and feedback — the descriptions, attachments and correspondence you submit.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in and session records — time, IP address, sign-in method, success or failure and the reason for failure.
  • Device and client information — device identifier and model, operating system and browser version, user agent.
  • Approximate location — country, region and city derived from your IP address using a local GeoIP database. We do not collect GPS-precise location.
  • Operation audit — the administrative actions you perform in the console, with their target, time and outcome.
  • Security events — firewall interceptions, risk assessment outcomes, rate limiting and ban records.

1.3 Payment information

When you use paid features we record the order number, amount, currency, channel, status and time. We neither collect nor store your full card number, CVV or payment password — those are captured and processed directly by the payment channel.

3. How We Share It

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in the following situations, and only to the extent needed for the stated purpose:

  1. Processors — third parties engaged to deliver the service, including cloud compute and database providers, object storage providers, email and SMS providers, and payment channels. We enter into data processing agreements requiring them to act on our instructions and apply appropriate safeguards.
  2. Third-party sign-in providers — where you choose to sign in with a third-party account, we request the identifier and basic profile we need. Authorising this is your choice, and you can unlink at any time from the console.
  3. Your organisation — your membership, roles and operation audit are visible to administrators of the organisation you belong to. This is inherent to organisational governance.
  4. Legal requirements — where compelled by law or by a judicial or administrative authority. Where legally permitted, we will notify you before disclosing.
  5. Corporate transactions — on a merger, acquisition or transfer of assets, we will inform the recipient of this policy and require it to continue to be bound. Any change to this policy is notified under section 9.

4. International Transfers

We may use infrastructure and third-party services located outside your country or region. Where a transfer takes place we apply the safeguards required by applicable law, which may include standard contractual clauses, a transfer impact assessment, or obtaining your separate consent. You can request a description of the mechanisms currently in use at {{contactEmail}}.

5. Retention

  • Account and profile information — kept while the account exists; deleted after closure on the schedule in the Terms of Service.
  • Sign-in and session records — at least 6 months, for security traceability.
  • Operation audit — at least 12 months.
  • Transaction and receipt records — as required by applicable accounting and tax law, typically at least 5 years.
  • Risk assessment records — 90 days by default; administrators can configure the purge policy in the console.

Beyond these periods we delete or anonymise. Where immediate deletion is technically impracticable, we stop all processing other than storage and the security measures necessary to protect it.

6. How We Protect It

  • Passwords are salted and hashed — we cannot read your plaintext password. Two-factor secrets and third-party credentials are encrypted under a separate master key.
  • HTTPS is enforced in transit; the application gateway additionally offers signed and end-to-end encrypted tiers.
  • Access follows least privilege, and administrative actions are recorded end to end.
  • A web application firewall, replay protection, rate limiting and a risk engine identify and block anomalous access.
  • Backups are taken regularly and restores are rehearsed; backups carry the same encryption and access controls.

Please understand that no safeguard is absolute. In the event of a personal data breach we will notify the competent supervisory authority within the period required by applicable law, and inform affected users promptly, describing the impact and the measures taken.

7. Your Rights

Within the limits of applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Be informed and obtain access — understand how we process your information and receive a copy. The console's profile and activity pages are the everyday route.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete information, which you can do directly in the console.
  • Erasure — request deletion where the purpose has been fulfilled, you withdraw consent, or we have processed unlawfully.
  • Restriction and objection — request restriction in defined circumstances, or object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Portability — receive the information you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, or have it transferred to another controller.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time for processing based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Explanation — ask us to explain our processing rules.
  • Complain — to us, or to a competent supervisory authority.

Exercise these rights through the corresponding console feature or by writing to {{contactEmail}}. We respond within 15 working days of verifying your identity. There is no charge, though we may charge a reasonable fee or decline where a request is manifestly unfounded or excessively repetitive.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use browser local storage and cookies to keep you signed in and to remember interface preferences such as sidebar width, colour theme and recently visited pages. We do not use third-party advertising cookies and we do not perform cross-site behavioural analysis. You can clear this data in your browser, but you will need to sign in again and preferences will return to their defaults.

9. Children

The management console is intended for administrators at companies and organisations and is not directed at children. If we learn that we have collected a child's personal information without guardian consent, we will delete it promptly. Where an integrator's end users include children, that integrator is responsible for obtaining guardian consent and for the corresponding protections.

For material changes — a broadened purpose, additional recipients, or a change in how you exercise your rights — we will give at least 7 days' notice before they take effect via console announcement, in-product message or email, and will seek fresh consent where required. The effective date of the current version is always shown at the top of this page.
